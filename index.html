08 // FAQ BEFORE YOU ASK

GOOD TO
KNOW

How long does a project take?

A landing page usually ships in 3–5 days, a full business website in 7–10 days, and larger stores or apps in 2–4 weeks. You get a firm date in writing before we start.

How do payments work?

50% to book your slot and start work, 50% before launch. For monthly care plans it's billed at the start of each month. Bank transfer, UPI, PayPal or Stripe — whatever's easiest for you.

Do I need to provide content and images?

Ideally yes — you know your business best. But if you're stuck, we'll help structure the copy and source premium stock imagery so nothing holds up the launch.

What if I don't like the design?

You see the design before a single line of code is written, and every package includes revision rounds. Nothing gets built until you're genuinely happy with how it looks.

Will I be able to update it myself?

Yes. Business packages ship with an easy editor for text and images, plus a short walkthrough video recorded for your exact site.

Do you work with clients outside India?

All the time. Most work happens over email and scheduled calls, and we keep flexible hours to overlap with US, UK and Gulf time zones.
